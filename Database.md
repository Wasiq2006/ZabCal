# Faculty Of Management Sciences

Domain Elective = 3 Credit Hours
University Elective = 3 Credit Hours

SZABIST offers a four-year (eight semesters) BBA degree program of 146 credit hours (An Additional
02 Credit Hours for Muslim Students). This is SZABIST’s flagship program. For the BBA program,
students are required to complete 49 courses and 03 credit hours Business Project, a 02 credit hours
Community Service Project and 03 credit hours Graded Internship. The students will take 4 Program
Electives courses from Management, Marketing, Finance, Business Analytics, IT or Supply Chain
Management. All Muslim undergraduate students admitted in Fall 2025 and onwards will be required
to complete two compulsory one-credit-hour courses on the understanding of Holy Quran as part
of their degree requirements. The maximum time limit for degree completion is six years, offering
flexibility to learners. The BBA program is accredited by the ACCA and the National Business
Education Accreditation Council (NBEAC) and is also affiliated with the Chartered Financial Analyst
(CFA) Institute.
