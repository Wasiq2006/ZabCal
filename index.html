About ZabCal

person

Built for SZABIST Students

Hi, I’m Wasiq.

I built ZabCal because I wanted a fast, local-first CGPA and SGPA calculator that feels simple on mobile and clean on desktop.

Local First

Everything stays in your browser.

Semester Tracking

Save SGPA history per term.

Mobile Ready

Designed to work smoothly on small screens.

Why this exists

There wasn’t a dedicated tool that felt quick enough for everyday course entry, so ZabCal was built to keep calculations readable, accurate, and low-friction.

The goal is practical: help students check CGPA, SGPA, semester history, and progress without losing time to clutter or manual calculations.

Version 2.3.0 Changes

Theme:

Overhauled dark theme on mobile & desktop with modern glassmorphism, crisp contrast, and refined surfaces.

Mobile UI:

Replaced wide tables with native mobile course cards, 2x2 Quick Stats grid, and touch-optimized bottom navigation.

Electives:

Added 180+ Domain & University Electives for CS, SE, AI, and BBA with program-specific filtering.

Grading:

Enforced SZABIST Grade Replacement policy: Repeated courses count highest attempt in CGPA and credits once.

Added in-place course marks editing inside saved semesters with live SGPA recalculation.
