About ZabCal

person

Built for SZABIST Students

Hi, I’m Wasiq.

I built ZabCal because I wanted a fast, local-first CGPA and SGPA calculator that feels simple on mobile and clean on desktop.

Local First

Everything stays in your browser.

Semester Tracking

Save SGPA history per term.

Mobile Ready

Designed to work smoothly on small screens.

Why this exists

There wasn’t a dedicated tool that felt quick enough for everyday course entry, so ZabCal was built to keep calculations readable, accurate, and low-friction.

The goal is practical: help students check CGPA, SGPA, semester history, and progress without losing time to clutter or manual calculations.
